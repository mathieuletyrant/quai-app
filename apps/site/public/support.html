Getting started

01

Install Quai Home

With Docker, on your own server. Your data stays there.

02

Choose a Quai Cloud

Link to ours, or run your own and point Quai Home to it with QUAI_CLOUD_URL.

03

Check your services

In the console under Services, each one should read Connected.

04

Pair your iPhone

Under iPhones, show the QR code and scan it from the app.

Troubleshooting

No Live Activity shows up

Check that Live Activities are on in iPhone Settings › Quai, that the flow is Enabled in the console under Flows, and that Quai Cloud reads Connected at the bottom of the console. Then use the flow’s Test button to send one to your iPhone.

The app doesn’t connect away from home

With our Quai Cloud, there is nothing to set: check that Quai Cloud reads Connected in the console’s Settings. With your own, set QUAI_CLOUD_PUBLIC_URL (and QUAI_CLOUD_PUBLIC_HEADERS if your proxy asks for them) on Quai Cloud, then pair the iPhone again: both go into the QR code.

“Open the console” doesn’t work away from home

Set QUAI_CONSOLE_PUBLIC_URL on Quai Home to the console’s address from outside (behind your own proxy or tunnel), then pair the iPhone again. The app opens the console at home when it answers, and this address when it doesn’t.

A service isn’t connected

Check its internal address and API key under Services. A service marked Env is set by Quai Home’s environment variables (such as QUAI_RADARR_URL), which win over the page: change it there.

Seerr requests arrive late

If Seerr’s webhook already posts somewhere else, Quai leaves it as it is and checks requests regularly instead. The Journal says so when it happens.

A Live Activity updates slowly

iOS limits how often Live Activities refresh, more so in Low Power Mode. Quai sends an update at each step change and regularly during a download.

I don’t get alerts

Alerts go to every iPhone that allows Quai’s notifications. Check iPhone Settings › Notifications › Quai, then the mute options in the app’s Settings, then the alert’s switch in the console under Alerts.

How do I remove Quai?

Remove each iPhone under iPhones, then uninstall Quai Home: it removes its webhooks from your services. Delete the app from your iPhone.

In a bug report, please include

  • the app build number, from TestFlight;
  • your Quai Home version, shown at the bottom of the console;
  • self-hosted Quai Cloud or ours;
  • what you did, and what you expected;
  • the Journal lines from around that time, if any.

Beta limits

  • Playback works with Jellyfin, not Plex or Emby.
  • iPhone only.
  • TestFlight builds expire after 90 days; new ones install on their own.
