Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Quai iPhone app, Quai Home and our Quai Cloud.

In short

  • Your service addresses, API keys, library and journal stay on your own server, in Quai Home.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDK.
  • If you use our Quai Cloud, it only relays Live Activity updates to Apple. Self-host it and nothing goes through us.

Who we are

Quai is made by Mathieu Le Tyrant, an independent developer based in France (“we”). We are the data controller for the little data our Quai Cloud handles. For anything in this policy, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

How Quai is built

Quai has three parts. Where your data goes depends on which of them you run yourself.

PARTWHERE IT RUNS
Quai HomeAlways on your server, next to your media apps.
Quai CloudOurs, or yours if you self-host it. It runs the flows and sends pushes to Apple.
iPhone appOn your iPhone, paired to your Quai Home.

Quai Home: on your server

Quai Home stores the addresses and API keys of your services (Radarr, Sonarr, Prowlarr, qBittorrent, Seerr, Jellyfin, Cleanuparr, Reclaimerr), your flows and alert settings, the list of paired iPhones, and a journal of what Quai did, kept for 7 days. All of this stays on your hardware. We have no access to it, and Quai Home never sends addresses or keys to any Quai Cloud.

Our Quai Cloud: only if you link to it

To start and update your Live Activities and alerts, our Quai Cloud receives from your Quai Home:

  • the content of each update: for example a title, an episode number, the current step, a progress percentage, or the name of a service that is down;
  • the Apple push tokens of your paired iPhones, so it can address them.

It forwards updates to Apple Push Notification service and does not keep their content once sent. It keeps push tokens only while the iPhone is paired [CONFIRM: storage and duration]. It never receives your service addresses, API keys, library or journal. If you run Quai Cloud yourself, none of this reaches us.

The iPhone app

The app keeps on your iPhone the pairing details read from the QR code (your Quai Home’s local and public addresses, and Cloudflare Access headers, stored in the Keychain) and your preferences, such as which Live Activities and alerts you receive. It talks only to your Quai Home.

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK, and it does not track you across apps or websites. It does not access your contacts, photos or location.

Apple and TestFlight

Live Activities and notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service, under Apple’s privacy policy.

During the beta, TestFlight shares with us your name and email address if you were invited by email, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshot you choose to send. We use them only to fix and improve Quai.

If you reach Quai Home through Cloudflare Tunnel and Access, that traffic goes through your own Cloudflare account and its terms.

Sharing

We don’t sell or rent any data, and we don’t share it with anyone except the providers needed to run our Quai Cloud: Apple, to deliver pushes, and [HOSTING PROVIDER], which hosts our Quai Cloud in [REGION].

Retention and deletion

  • Remove an iPhone in the console under iPhones: its pairing and push token are deleted.
  • Unlink Quai Cloud in the console’s Settings: our Quai Cloud stops receiving anything.
  • Uninstall Quai Home: its data is deleted and its webhooks are removed from your services.
  • Delete the app: everything it stored on your iPhone goes with it.
  • To delete beta feedback you sent us, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete data about you, or object to its use, by writing to [SUPPORT EMAIL]. You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr).

Quai is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and important changes are mentioned in the TestFlight release notes. Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].
