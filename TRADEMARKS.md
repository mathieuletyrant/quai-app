logo are not: the license explicitly grants no rights to them (section 6).

- run, modify and self-host Quai, and build the iOS app for your own use, with your own Apple
  developer account, bundle id and push key;
- say that your project is "based on Quai" or "compatible with Quai".

**You may not**, without written permission
- publish an app, image or service named "Quai", or with a name or logo that could be mistaken
  for it;
- use the Quai logo, or the `com.mathieuletyrant.quai` bundle id.

A fork that ships to others needs its own name, logo and bundle id.

The official iOS app on the App Store and the hosted push relay are provided by the author.
